Research

My research focuses on exoplanet characterization, with a particular emphasis on atmospheric modeling and the development of tools for future space missions. I use advanced techniques in atmospheric retrieval, radiative transfer modeling, and data analysis to study distant worlds and contribute to our understanding of planetary habitability.

Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO)

As part of the HWO project, I'm developing Multi-bandpass Photometry for Exoplanet Atmosphere Reconnaissance (MPEAR). This work focuses on optimizing observing strategies for detecting and characterizing Earth-like planets around Sun-like stars.

Key achievements:

  • Developed a new algorithm for determining optimal broadband photometric observations
  • Created a noise and exposure time calculator for the HWO coronagraph instrument
  • Demonstrated the capability to differentiate Earth-like planets from Neptunes using multi-bandpass observations

Large Interferometer For Exoplanets (LIFE)

I'm actively involved in the LIFE mission, focusing on atmospheric retrievals and defining mission requirements. My work helps quantify the potential of LIFE for characterizing Earth-like exoplanets and their atmospheres.

Research highlights:

  • Performed Bayesian retrievals on simulated spectra of Earth at various evolutionary stages
  • Assessed the impact of clouds on atmospheric retrievals
  • Investigated the synergy between LIFE and other future space missions like HWO

Exo-MerCat

I developed Exo-MerCat, a comprehensive merged catalog of exoplanets with Virtual Observatory connection. This tool has significantly improved the accessibility and usability of exoplanet data for researchers worldwide.

Key features:

  • Integration of multiple exoplanet databases
  • VO-aware Python code for easy data retrieval
  • Standardized output and notation differences handling
  • Regular updates to include newly discovered exoplanets

Atmospheric Retrieval Studies

My work in atmospheric retrieval focuses on developing and refining techniques to extract maximum information from exoplanet spectra. This research is crucial for future space missions and our understanding of exoplanet atmospheres.

Research focus:

  • Investigating the impact of different opacity tables on retrieval results
  • Assessing the effects of clouds on atmospheric characterization
  • Studying the potential of combining data from multiple missions (e.g., LIFE and HWO)
